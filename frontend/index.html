Chest Pain Types What does each option mean?
Typical Angina

Chest discomfort commonly associated with reduced blood flow to the heart.

Common: pressure, tightness or discomfort that may spread to the arm, neck or jaw.
Atypical Angina

Chest discomfort that has some features of angina but does not follow the usual pattern.

Symptoms can occur differently from typical angina.
Non-anginal Pain

Chest pain that does not fit the typical pattern associated with angina.

Chest discomfort can have many possible causes.
Asymptomatic

No noticeable chest pain or related symptoms are reported.

Lack of symptoms does not by itself rule out a health condition.
